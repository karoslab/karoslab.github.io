GARADI.

A private fitness accountability app with a coach that adapts to your routine. Track nutrition, weight, and activity — and get pushed at exactly the intensity you choose.

What is Garadi?

Garadi keeps you honest. Log your food and weight, pull in your daily activity from Apple Health, and let your coach hold the line. The coach adapts to your routine and to the intensity you pick — from a gentle nudge to a no-excuses drill sergeant — so accountability feels like it fits you, not the other way around.

Everything stays private: your data lives on your device and, if you choose, in your own iCloud. There is no Garadi server collecting your information.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the coach profiles?

The coach has four intensity profiles you can switch between any time:

  • Friendly — warm, encouraging, low pressure.
  • Supporter — steady cheerleading with a bit more structure.
  • Scolder — sharper, calls you out when you slip.
  • Locked In — maximum intensity, no excuses, hard mode.

If a profile is too much on a given day, use Dial Down to temporarily soften the coach's tone without losing your settings.

Can I get daily reminders?

Yes. Garadi can send daily reminders to log your meals, weigh-ins, and check your activity. Enable notifications when prompted, or turn them on later in the iOS Settings app under Notifications → Garadi. You can adjust or turn off reminders in the app at any time.

What is Garadi Pro, and is there a free trial?

Garadi Pro unlocks the full coaching experience and premium features. Some new subscribers get a free trial; the length is shown before you subscribe. You won't be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel any time before then to avoid being billed.

How do I cancel my subscription or trial?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, so you cancel through your Apple ID settings:

On your iPhone, open Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → select Garadi → Cancel Subscription (or Cancel Free Trial). You keep access until the end of the current billing period or trial.

How does my data and iCloud sync work?

Your nutrition, weight, and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData. If you enable private sync, Garadi backs up and restores that data through your own private iCloud (Apple CloudKit), tied to your Apple ID.

Your data stays inside your own iCloud account — there is no Garadi server collecting it, and it is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.

What HealthKit permissions does Garadi need?

With your permission, Garadi reads activity data from Apple Health — step count, active energy, exercise time, and workouts — to show your daily activity alongside your calories, macros, and weight. Reading is used only inside the app; it is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining.

Saving to Health is separate and off by default. If you turn on “Save food and weight to Health” in Settings, Garadi also asks to save your logged calories, protein, carbs and fat (with the food's name), and your weigh-ins, to Apple Health. Switching it off keeps what was already saved; “Remove what Garadi saved to Health” deletes only Garadi's own entries from that device. If another app also saves your meals to Health, they can be counted twice.

You can review or revoke access any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Garadi.

How do I delete my data?

To remove locally stored data, use the in-app deletion tools, or delete the app from your device. To remove synced data, delete the app's data from iCloud (Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage). Because there is no Garadi server, deleting your local and iCloud data removes it everywhere. If you'd like help, email us at karthikhsg@gmail.com.
